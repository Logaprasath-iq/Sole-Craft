Pricing FAQs & Details

How do you estimate rates for custom repairs?

Rates are estimated based on material damage, structural requirements (e.g. Goodyear welting vs blake stitching), and required leather conditioning oils. We provide transparent estimates via photos or in-person review.

Is there a warranty on stitching and soles?

Yes! All full resoling projects and major stitching works include a 1-year warranty. If you experience structural issues under normal wear, we repair it free of charge.

Can you restore shoes that are badly cracked?

While deeply cracked leather cannot be restored to "brand new", we utilize deep hydration baths and premium fillers to fill cracks, stop further tearing, and restore color patina.

How long does a standard service take?

Standard maintenance takes 1-2 days, while full structural resoles and custom patina dyeing require 5-7 days to allow correct curing and drying of organic adhesives.
