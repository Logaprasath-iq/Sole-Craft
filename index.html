Our Restoration Method

The Sole Craft Process

1

Send & Diagnose

Book online and send or drop off your pair. Our master cobblers conduct a detailed structural inspection to assess sole strength and leather hydration.

2

Artisan Crafting

We perform deep cleaning, hand-stitch repairs, reconstruct corkbeds, and apply new outsoles using century-old bespoke techniques.

3

Polish & Return

After curing, we finish your shoes with premium gold-medal waxes to build a deep, protected patina. Your pair is returned looking refreshed.
