Our Story

Built on Craftsmanship

For over 15 years, Sole Craft has stood as Delhi’s premier bespoke restoration house. We believe that fine footwear is an investment in comfort, style, and heritage—one that should be preserved, not discarded.

Restoring Heritage, Step by Step

Sole Craft was founded by Arthur Cobble, a third-generation cobbler who realized that modern mass-market culture had forgotten the value of high-quality shoe construction. What began as a tiny, two-person workshop has grown into a state-of-the-art restoration studio, servicing luxury shoes from all over the country.

Our values remain unchanged: uncompromising attention to detail, sourcing the world's finest leather materials (such as French Calfskin and Italian Vibram rubber outsoles), and treating every pair as a unique piece of industrial art.

Our Mission

To extend the lifecycle of premium footwear, reducing environmental waste while maintaining ultimate foot comfort.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as the golden standard for bespoke leather footwear restoration and repair.
